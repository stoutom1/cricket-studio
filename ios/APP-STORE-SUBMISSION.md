## Description
Cric4All brings cricket scoring, live match viewing, league management and match-day tools together in one place.

Score matches ball by ball with support for cricket scoring workflows including innings, extras, wickets, striker rotation, bowling changes, match results and detailed scorecards.

Keep scoring resilient when connectivity is unreliable. Cric4All can preserve supported in-progress scoring data on the device and synchronize queued scoring activity when connectivity returns.

Follow matches through spectator views, share match and live-score links, review completed matches, and keep league and team information organized.

Cric4All also includes tools for league administrators and scorers, with features available according to the user's league permissions.

Key capabilities include:
- Ball-by-ball cricket scoring
- Live spectator scoring views
- Offline scoring continuity and reconnect synchronization
- Match scorecards and results
- League and team management
- Player and match statistics
- Match sharing
- Match and league alerts where enabled
- Role-based league access

Some features require an internet connection, an account, league access, or administrator/scorer permissions.
